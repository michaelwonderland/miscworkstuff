Phase 01 · Foundations
02 · Foundations

The plumbing, done once
and done right.

Most of what makes paid media work is decided before the first ad goes live. In the first two weeks we connect Shopify and Klaviyo to Meta and Google, so the platforms see accurate sales, know who your customers already are, and have a clean catalogue to advertise from.

Measurement

Meta Pixel with Conversions API and Google Ads with Enhanced Conversions, both server-side via Shopify and deduplicated. Consent Mode v2, GA4 aligned, one UTM taxonomy. The platforms optimise on real purchases; we report on Shopify's numbers.

Product feed

One feed from Shopify into Merchant Center and the Meta catalogue. Titles rewritten the way people shop for coffee: origin, process, roast and tasting notes. Labels for bestsellers, new releases, subscriptions and margin, so spend goes where it should.

Audience segments

Built from Shopify order history and Klaviyo engagement: subscribers, repeat buyers, one-time buyers, lapsing customers, and engaged email readers who haven't bought yet. Each segment has a job: exclude, retain, win back, or seed lookalikes.

Data syncs

Klaviyo and Shopify segments sync automatically to Meta Custom Audiences and Google Customer Match, so audiences stay current without anyone re-uploading lists. New-customer goals on both platforms weight spend towards people who've never bought from you.

03 · Launch

Meta and Google,
in Workshop's voice.

The first campaigns follow current best practice on both platforms: simple structures, broad targeting led by creative, and feed-led shopping. The guardrails below keep the work adding to the brand rather than spending it down.

i.Product and craft first

Creative built from the coffee, the roasting, the cafés and the people behind them, not from discount codes.

ii.Offers used deliberately

First-order and subscription incentives are tested as levers, not left running as the default.

iii.Frequency kept in check

Caps on retargeting, so no one sees the same ad a dozen times in a week.

iv.The name protected

Brand search covered, so competitors and resellers don't sit above Workshop when people search for you.

Performance Marketing Management

Meta & Google · Proposal · November 2026

The engagement

Workshop has the brand, the product and the customers, and no performance marketing yet. We'd build it from the ground up: the data foundations first, then Meta and Google run to best practice, in a way that adds to the brand rather than spending it down.

Three months to set up, launch and find what works on a £15k initial test budget, then scale it.

Scope
Phase 01

Performance marketing foundations

  • Measurement: server-side tracking on Meta (Conversions API) and Google (Enhanced Conversions), deduplicated, consent-ready, one UTM taxonomy
  • Product feed: one Shopify feed into Merchant Center and the Meta catalogue, titles written the way people shop for coffee
  • Audience segments: subscribers, repeat, one-time, lapsing and engaged-not-bought, each with a job: exclude, retain, win back or seed
  • Data syncs: Klaviyo and Shopify segments refreshed automatically into Meta and Google, with new-customer goals on both
Phase 02

Running Meta & Google

  • First campaigns built and launched: Meta prospecting and catalogue, Google Shopping / PMax, non-brand and brand search
  • Creative led by product and craft, offers used deliberately, frequency capped. Ads that look like Workshop
  • Day-to-day management, budget allocation and pacing across both channels, with QA before anything goes live
  • Weekly iterations, the most frequent rhythm we recommend, scaling budget into what works

## 2. Consensus

**Role and economics**
- Statics are the cheapest, fastest creative to test, and the natural first layer for finding messages before paying for video. `alessio-cordeddu/knowledge/frameworks/creative-flooding.md §Step 1` · `phil-kiel/knowledge/themes/static-ads-and-teardowns.md §principle 4, 6` · `dara-denney/knowledge/frameworks/creative-testing-protocol.md §Tier 1` · `marin-istvanic/knowledge/frameworks/creative-diversification-method.md §3` · `lorenzo-pravata/knowledge/frameworks/ai-production-pipelines.md §Pipeline 1 v1` ("cavalry").
- "Statics are only for retargeting" is wrong; built for a specific awareness level, they prospect. `dara-denney/knowledge/frameworks/static-ad-construction.md §Three myths` · `alessio-cordeddu/knowledge/themes/static-ads.md §The position` · `phil-kiel/knowledge/themes/static-ads-and-teardowns.md §principle 5` (see split 1 for the conditions).
- Run statics alongside video, not instead of it: a mediocre static beats a mediocre video, the best video beats the best static, and most accounts now run both together. `ben-heath/knowledge/themes/creative-and-hooks.md §Format and style` · `phil-kiel/knowledge/themes/static-ads-and-teardowns.md §principle 6` (50:50 pairing) · `nick-theriot/knowledge/themes/creative-formats.md` (format monogamy is the error).

**Construction rules**
- One ad, one job, one core problem. `dara-denney/knowledge/frameworks/static-ad-construction.md §Layer 1` · `phil-kiel/knowledge/themes/static-ads-and-teardowns.md §What the teardowns praise` ("names one core problem") and §punish ("two variables at once") · `alessio-cordeddu/knowledge/themes/static-ads.md §17` (one mechanism per ad).
- Clarity in about a second: a stranger must know what's sold and to whom. `dara-denney/.../static-ad-construction.md §Layer 2` (1-second test) · `phil-kiel/.../static-ads-and-teardowns.md §principle 1-3` (product legibility; remove-the-text test) · `alessio-cordeddu/.../static-ads.md §Copy` ("what's in it for me" failure; not showing the product).
- The headline does the work, the image stops the scroll; keep text short. `dara-denney/.../static-ad-construction.md §Layer 2` (headline is focal point 9/10) · `alessio-cordeddu/.../static-ads.md §Copy` (avoid the third line) · `phil-kiel/.../static-ads-and-teardowns.md §What the teardowns punish` ("too much to say") · `barry-hott/knowledge/themes/ugly-ads.md §Applying it` (tighter crop, bigger text, headline near top).
- Native beats polished; brand styling telegraphs "ad". `barry-hott/knowledge/themes/ugly-ads.md §What it actually means` · `alessio-cordeddu/.../static-ads.md §Category 5` · `phil-kiel/.../static-ads-and-teardowns.md §punish` ("If your ads are too perfect...") · `dara-denney/knowledge/themes/static-and-ai-creative.md §Retired` (brand fonts "scream ad"). Note: native-looking is not the same as careless (see `CONCEPTS.md creative.ugly_ad`).
- Best copy is found in customer language, and it must sound like a person, not a billboard. `dara-denney/.../static-ad-construction.md §Layer 3` (review mining) · `alessio-cordeddu/.../static-ads.md §8` (quote beats brand headline; polished quotes fail) · `phil-kiel/.../static-ads-and-teardowns.md §punish` ("Who thinks a customer actually wrote this?").
- Stack concepts in one image rather than one template per ad. `alessio-cordeddu/.../static-ads.md §29-concept taxonomy` ("you stack them") · `phil-kiel/.../static-ads-and-teardowns.md §praise` ("4x combo multiplier": 3 reasons why + Apple Notes + feature callout + press logos).
- Check safe zones on every design. `phil-kiel/.../static-ads-and-teardowns.md §Safe zones` · `alessio-cordeddu/.../static-ads.md §Copy` (ignoring safe zones listed as a failure).

**Diversity over near-duplicates (the flooding question)**
- Volume only helps when the statics are genuinely different; headline swaps on one image are not diversity. `alessio-cordeddu/.../creative-flooding.md §Strategic flooding` ("100 variations of the same mediocre idea") · `phil-kiel/knowledge/themes/creative-strategy-and-diversity.md §Current tactic` (tiny variations won't get spend, ~90% fail) · `lorenzo-pravata/knowledge/themes/creative-diversity-over-volume.md` ("Take one static image, change the headline a thousand times" is the wrong lever) · `dara-denney/.../creative-testing-protocol.md` (2-3 variants per concept, the rest "really big swings").
- Hypothesis first, then volume inside each hypothesis; read the result by message, then make video for the winners. `alessio-cordeddu/.../creative-flooding.md §Steps 1-3` · `marin-istvanic/.../creative-diversification-method.md §3` (statics → video → advertorial; validate against cold audiences) · `dara-denney/.../creative-testing-protocol.md §Tier 1`.

## 3. Splits

**Split 1. Can a static prospect cold, or does it mostly retarget?**
- **Camp A (statics prospect):** Dara: built to an awareness level and narrow persona, a static reaches cold audiences and scales (`dara-denney/.../static-ad-construction.md §Three myths`). Alessio: problem callout "works cold" (`alessio-cordeddu/.../static-ads.md §2`). Phil: statics are the first format on a new account (`phil-kiel/.../static-ads-and-teardowns.md §principle 6`). Wins for self-explanatory products and when the headline does the awareness work.
- **Camp B (images mostly remind):** Marin (2026-08): for problem-solving products (supplements, skincare, software) "videos prospect, images retarget"; image frequency above 2.0 means you're reaching people who know you; self-explanatory products (clothing, jewelry, toys) are the exception (`marin-istvanic/knowledge/evolution.md §10`; `frameworks/creative-diversification-method.md`). Lorenzo: statics hit a ceiling if the product takes over 10 seconds to explain (`lorenzo-pravata/.../ai-production-pipelines.md §Pipeline 1 v1`). Nick: statics still work but rank below B-roll/captions video in most accounts (`nick-theriot/.../creative-formats.md` item 7).
- **What it's about:** product type and how much education the sale needs, not the format.
- **Suggested verdict:** if a stranger understands the product from one image and one line, statics can carry prospecting. If it needs explaining, use statics to test messages and video to educate, and check image-ad frequency and new-visitor share before calling a static a prospecting winner.

**Split 2. Should you remake competitors' long-running statics?**
- **Camp A (yes):** Lorenzo calls the competitor remake of a static running 90+ days "the highest-leverage" prompt (`lorenzo-pravata/prompts/gpt-image-static-ad-prompts.md §1`). Ben: 3+ months live is a strong (not sufficient) success signal (`ben-heath/knowledge/frameworks/ads-library-modeling.md`). Nick filters reference ads at 30+ days running (`nick-theriot/.../creative-formats.md §Native ads`).
- **Camp B (no):** Dara retired the longevity heuristic in 2025-04 (long-running ads are often "buried in an ASC+ campaign no one's touched") and calls copying competitors "the worst creative strategy" (2026-07) (`dara-denney/knowledge/evolution.md §3a, 3b`). Phil and Barry add cautions: don't take top-of-funnel inspiration from mature brands or brands that scaled 5+ years ago (`phil-kiel/.../static-ads-and-teardowns.md §Competitor teardowns`; `barry-hott/.../ugly-ads.md §Applying it`).
- **What it's about:** evidence standards. Ad Library run time is a weak proxy for performance.
- **Suggested verdict:** borrow layouts as structure, never as proof. A remake is a hypothesis to test, not a shortcut past one. Prefer your own winners as the source (first-frame screenshots of your top videos are pre-validated; `dara-denney/knowledge/frameworks/7-lazy-formulas.md §2`).

**Split 3. Before/after statics.**
- **Camp A:** Dara: transformation is the biggest gap in most accounts; before/after is not illegal, restrictions apply to cosmetic-procedure and weight-loss (`dara-denney/.../static-ad-construction.md §Layer 3, format library`). Alessio: "your eye does the math" (#6-7).
- **Camp B:** Nick downgraded before/after to C-tier after an acne test where after-only beat before/after; flags ban risk and lists restricted body parts (`nick-theriot/.../creative-formats.md §Before/after`). Ben: use sequential, not split-image, for body transformations (`ben-heath/.../creative-and-hooks.md`).
- **Suggested verdict:** run the transformation idea, but check category policy first; in restricted categories test after-only or time-progression ("day 2 / day 42") versions. Verify against Meta's policy docs (Open questions).

**Split 4. Should visibly-AI statics ship?**
- **Camp A (AI-looking is a legitimate register):** Barry: "AI slop" resembles what consumers already consume (`barry-hott/.../ugly-ads.md §Current position`). Dara: outrageous AI-character ads work because they don't pretend to be real (`dara-denney/.../static-and-ai-creative.md §Part 2`). Alessio: animation is "honest about being fake" and safe to scale (`alessio-cordeddu/.../ai-and-tooling.md`).
- **Camp B (if it looks AI, burn it):** Nick (`nick-theriot/knowledge/themes/ai-and-tooling.md §the one rule`). Alessio on realistic AI: not competing with a real person shot well, plus regulatory exposure. Barry himself warns AI static images face a supply/consumption mismatch and users will habituate (`barry-hott/knowledge/themes/ai-and-tooling.md §skeptical`).
- **What it's about:** stylized vs fake-realistic. Openly stylized AI (cartoon, animation) has support; realistic AI passed off as real does not.
- **Suggested verdict:** use AI for openly stylized statics or for product-accurate imagery a human has checked; don't ship realistic AI people or results that a viewer could mistake for real.

**Split 5. Is AI output a finished static or a mockup?**
- **Camp A (finished, after QA):** Lorenzo (2026-05): GPT Image 2 can "one shot great statics… instead of having to edit in canva." Dara: Claude brand-context doc → generation tool, 3 variations at a time, human QA pass (`dara-denney/.../static-and-ai-creative.md §What she actually runs`).
- **Camp B (mockup or input):** Phil (2025-06): AI is an ideation mockup, "AI asset creation when required" (`phil-kiel/.../platform-mechanics-and-bugs.md §AI creative`). Alessio (2025-04): never let the model render final ad text; generate text-free visuals, add copy after (`alessio-cordeddu/.../ai-and-tooling.md`). Nick: a designer produces the static with AI tools (`nick-theriot/.../ai-and-tooling.md`).
- **What it's about:** model capability, which moved between 2025 and 2026. The older positions predate the newer image models.
- **Suggested verdict:** treat AI as a draft generator. Ship only what passes the 1-second test and a product-accuracy check (labels, proportions, text).

## 4. Single-source insights
- Don't put anything that looks like a tappable button on a static; people try to tap it. Alessio, `static-ads.md §Anatomy`.
- Urgency must be tied to a real event; discontinuation framing beats discount. Alessio, `static-ads.md §14`.
- Statics with light animation (explainers, FAQs, steps, reviews) are a common gap. Phil, `static-ads-and-teardowns.md §Checklists`.
- Build a few ads in IG Stories using only text, stickers and emoji to break brand-book habits. Phil, `static-ads-and-teardowns.md §Current tactic`.
- Screenshot the first frame of your best videos (aim ~30) and run them as statics. Dara, `7-lazy-formulas.md §2`.
- Prompt AI image tools for whitespace (top for headline, bottom for CTA). Lorenzo, `ai-production-pipelines.md §Pipeline 1`.
- Lock product details in a structured description before generating, to cut label and proportion errors. Alessio, `ai-and-tooling.md` (2025-04).
- Meme-style text overlays on already-winning statics. Marin, `testing-and-iteration.md`.
- Never report hook rate for a static; read CPA, ROAS, spend, outbound CTR. Dara, `static-ad-construction.md §MCP step 6`.
- Older audiences (55+): chunky text, high contrast, low-fi, Facebook-native. Phil, `static-ads-and-teardowns.md §Current tactic`.
- Creative Enhancements on image ads: AI variants on but filtered; text enhancements and overlays off for text-heavy images. Ben, `creative-and-hooks.md §Creative Enhancements`.

## 5. Numbers
- **Statics per test:** 20-25 per ad set with 2-3 variants per concept (Dara, current); 10-20 concepts inside each messaging hypothesis (Alessio, 2026-03); 80 AI variations to find an angle (Lorenzo, 2026-04, sponsored single-account case: do not use the result figures). Marin indexes test volume to hit rate (`expert-panel/SKILL.md §Known live disagreements`).
- **Failure rate of near-duplicate statics:** ~90% (Phil, 2025-08/09).
- **Format share of winners:** video ~60%, image ~27%, carousel ~9% in 2025, from ~44/38/14 two years earlier (Ben's poll plus his account data; one source, flag as such).
- **Image frequency threshold:** above 2.0 suggests the image is reaching warm audiences (Marin, 2026-08; single source).
- **Text limits across the corpus:** 1-2 lines of same-style text (Alessio); ~4 feature callouts max (Dara); 3-4 press logos max (Alessio); 3 reasons ideal, 5 max (Alessio); 3-4 infographic steps (Lorenzo). Rough consensus: fewer elements than feels natural.
- **Production capacity:** a part-time designer produces 250-500 statics/month (Alessio; single source).
- **Old production cost being replaced:** $2,000-20,000 and 3+ weeks per round of video (Alessio, 2026-03).
- **Aspect ratios:** images 4:5 and 9:16, 1:1 for Flexible ads (Phil, 2026-02; "explicitly not universal"). Check Meta docs.

## 6. Myths and retired advice
- "Statics are for retargeting only." Rejected by Dara (2026-06), Alessio (since 2024), Phil (2025-01).
- "Image ads don't work for us." Usually means the specific images tried didn't (Phil, 2025-01); "a total scale issue" (Dara).
- Meta's 20% text rule: dead (Phil, 2024-10).
- "Before/after ads are illegal." No; category restrictions apply (Dara, repeated since 2023).
- "You can't scale outside clothing/jewelry with image ads" (Marin, 2025-02): superseded by his own product-type rule (2026-08).
- "Match format complexity to the size of the ask" (Ben, 2025-02): revised 2026-06; most advertisers now run both together.
- "Dump 50-100 ChatGPT image ads into an ASC and let spend sort them" (Dara, 2025-03): superseded by her own 3-at-a-time workflow and volume correction.
- "Segment statics by message across ad sets" (Dara): replaced Dec 2025 by all statics in one ad set.
- "200+ days running = winner" (Dara foundational): retired 2025-04.
- Failure patterns to stop: three or more stacked post-its with crossed-out props; plain Notes screenshot with nothing else; phone-call/notification UI ads (Dara, 2024-10); mock-UI ads "for advertisers" (Phil, 2025-08).

## 8. Draper angle
- Statics are the fastest format to make in volume, and every consensus rule above pushes toward more distinct statics per test (20-25 per ad set in one camp). Making them is now fast; launching them is the bottleneck. Use only profile claims: "17+ clicks per ad in Ads Manager, repeated for every variation" (state the rebuild assumption), "create once, deploy many", automatic naming from the creative file (useful for reading results by message hypothesis), pulling creative straight from Google Drive.
- Worked arithmetic, hypothetical inputs: 3 hypotheses × 8 statics = 24 ads; if each is rebuilt in 2 ad sets at 17+ clicks, that's 816+ clicks. Mark the duplicate-cost assumption.
- Belief match: "Creative volume wins" fits only with the diversity caveat. The article must not imply that more near-duplicates help; the panel is unanimous they don't.
- Don't claim Draper improves performance; it saves the launch time.

# Draper Ads blog: 12 articles answering the questions Meta advertisers ask

**Angle:** Meta advertisers get their questions answered by Draper. Each post answers one question that creative strategist Dara Denney answers in her public content (YouTube and X, 2023 to 2026, distilled in `michaelwonderland/aimarketingschool/experts/dara-denney`). Each one is targeted at a keyword with 10+ US monthly searches (DataForSEO, October 2026).

**Profile:** `draper-ads` (en-US). **Voice:** wonderblogwriter house voice (Julian Shapiro + Nat Eliason, Michael light).

## How the questions were found

1. **Her videos.** All 303 of Dara's indexed videos, ranked by views. The most-watched ones cluster on creative testing, hooks, ad formats, account structure, Andromeda, AI creative and catalog ads.
2. **Her knowledge base.** The 16 frameworks and 11 themes in the knowledge base, each read for the questions it answers.
3. **Candidate searches.** 147 searches an advertiser might type, checked for US search volume (Google Ads data).
4. **Real phrasings.** DataForSEO keyword suggestions on 18 seed topics: 1,411 keywords, 1,027 of them about paid social, filtered to 10+ searches.
5. **Top SERPs.** The top 10 results checked for each finalist. Most are Reddit threads, YouTube videos and thin agency posts: beatable.

## Rules for every post

- **Attribution:** Dara's distinctive frameworks, numbers and positions are attributed to her by name, with a link to the specific video (URLs are in her knowledge files). Write "creative strategist Dara Denney", never imply she endorses Draper, and never present her ideas as Draper's. General practitioner knowledge needs no citation.
- **Her disclosure rule:** she's Motion's Chief Evangelist. If a post cites a vendor-sourced stat she repeats (e.g. Marpipe's catalog figures), attribute it to the vendor.
- **Draper claims:** only those in `profiles/draper-ads.md`. Partnership ads are **not** a launched Draper feature. Don't say Draper supports them.
- **No invented first-hand data.** Draper has no published case data yet. Use worked arithmetic and Dara's cited examples instead, with at most one `[TK]` where first-hand proof would matter most.
- **Retired tactics:** never present anything from the "retired" sections of her files as current advice (e.g. ad longevity as proof of performance).
